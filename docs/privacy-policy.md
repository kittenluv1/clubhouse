# Privacy Policy & Data Architecture

## Overview

Clubhouse is a student-run discovery and review platform for UCLA clubs and campus organizations. This document details all data—specifically personally identifying information (PII) and activity signals—collected by Clubhouse, where that data is stored, how it is used, and who can access it.

Clubhouse is designed with student privacy in mind: public club reviews are published anonymously under random aliases, and user engagement signals (such as which clubs you saved or liked) are kept private.

---

## 1. When You Browse (Anonymous Visitors)

When visiting and searching Clubhouse without signing in:

- **Page and Interaction Analytics:** In production, PostHog captures anonymous pageviews (`$pageview`), club views (`club_viewed` with club ID and name), and category browsing to help us understand site navigation.
- **Search Queries:** Searches submitted via the search bar are sent to `/api/clubs` to perform trigram fuzzy search (`pg_trgm`). PostHog records the `club_searched` event with the search query term. Search queries are not tied to any personal identity.
- **Cookies & Storage:** Clubhouse does not store personal cookies for logged-out visitors. PostHog stores an anonymous distinct ID in client cookies/storage for session continuity. Third-party Google Identity Services (`gsi/client`) may initialize client scripts for one-tap sign-in.
- **Public Data Display:** Visitors can browse club details, view aggregated ratings, read approved anonymous reviews, and see total like counts.

---

## 2. When You Sign In (Account Data & PII)

Authentication is handled via **Google Identity Services** through **Supabase Auth**.

- **UCLA Email Address (`user_email`):** Retrieved from Google OAuth ID tokens. Clubhouse requires an authorized UCLA email domain (e.g., `@ucla.edu` or `@g.ucla.edu`). Stored in `auth.users` and attached to moderation records.
- **User Identifier (`user_id`):** A unique UUID generated by Supabase upon account creation (`auth.users.id`). It serves as the primary key linking a user's profile, likes, saves, and submitted reviews.
- **Session Tokens & Cookies:** Managed by `@supabase/ssr` via HttpOnly cookies (`src/proxy.js`) for authenticating requests to protected routes (`/profile`, `/review`, `/admin`).
- **Analytics Identity Linkage:** Upon successful sign-in, Clubhouse calls `posthog.identify(user.id, { email })` and emits `user_signed_in`. This connects past anonymous browsing on that device to the authenticated account.

## 3. Onboarding & Profile Preferences

When completing onboarding or updating profile settings (`/profile`):

- **Academic Information:**
  - `majors`: Up to 10 declared majors (stored in `profiles.majors`).
  - `minors`: Up to 10 declared minors (stored in `profiles.minors`).
- **Involvements:**
  - `current_clubs`: List of organizations the user is currently part of (stored in `profiles.current_clubs` and `club_memberships`).
- **Interests & Categories:**
  - Broad categories and subcategories selected by the student (stored in `user_interests` as `(user_id, category)` pairs).
- **Profile Metadata:**
  - `avatar_id`: A randomly assigned bear avatar ID (integer 1–7) used to visually represent the user without using their real photo.
  - `onboarding_started` / `onboarding_completed`: Flags tracking onboarding progress.
  - `last_viewed_rejected_at`: Timestamp tracking when the user last reviewed feedback on rejected reviews.

**Usage:** Profile data is used exclusively to personalize the user's dashboard and power the club recommendation algorithm. Profile information is **never** published publicly or sold.

## 4. User Activity & Engagement

- **Liked Clubs (`club_likes` table):**
  - Stores `(user_id, club_id)`.
  - **Public visibility:** Only the aggregate like count is displayed on club cards and club pages.
  - **Private visibility:** The specific list of clubs you liked is visible only to you on `/profile`. Who liked what is never revealed to other users.
- **Saved Clubs (`club_saves` table):**
  - Stores `(user_id, club_id)`.
  - Serves as private bookmarks. Only visible to you in `/profile`.
- **Review Likes (`review_likes` table):**
  - Stores `(user_id, review_id)` for helpful votes.
  - Only aggregate upvote counts are displayed publicly.
- **Club Memberships (`club_memberships` table):**
  - Stores `(user_id, club_id)`.
  - Tracks clubs the user belongs to; used to exclude those clubs from recommendation carousels.

## 5. Club Reviews & Anonymity Protections

Clubhouse enables candid student reviews while protecting reviewer safety:

- **Data Collected in Review Submission:**
  - Associated club (`club_id`, `club_name`)
  - Review text (`review_text`, up to 5,000 characters)
  - Quantitative ratings (1–5 scale in 0.5 increments): overall satisfaction, time commitment, inclusivity, social community, competitiveness
  - Membership dates (`membership_start_quarter/year`, `membership_end_quarter/year`)
  - Submitter metadata: `user_id`, `user_email`, auto-generated random `user_alias`
- **Moderation Workflow:**
  1. Reviews enter `pending_reviews`. A Supabase Edge Function triggers an automated notification email via Resend to the admin team and sends an acknowledgment email to the submitter.
  2. The admin panel (`/admin`) is restricted to authorized administrative emails to review submissions for compliance with Community Guidelines.
  3. Approved reviews move to the `reviews` table and trigger a database calculation updating the club's average satisfaction rating. Rejected reviews move to `rejected_reviews`.
- **Strict Anonymity Safeguards:**
  - `PUBLIC_REVIEW_FIELDS` strictly filters what is sent to clients (`src/app/lib/server/repositories/reviews.js`).
  - **Exposed to the public:** `club_id`, `club_name`, `review_text`, ratings, active quarter/year range, `user_alias`, `created_at`, and reviewer's random `avatar_id`.
  - **Never exposed to the public:** Neither `user_id` nor `user_email` is ever included in public review queries.
  - `is_own_review` is calculated server-side so that reviewers can manage their own reviews in `/profile` without broadcasting their identity.

## 6. How We Use the Data

1. **Service Delivery:** Authenticating accounts, saving bookmarks, maintaining review histories, and operating the review moderation queue.
2. **Personalized Recommendations:** The recommendation engine (`/api/recommendations`) uses a 7-factor weighted algorithm comparing candidate clubs against your major (20%), minor (9%), declared interests (16%), category preferences (13%), membership similarity (28%), like history (8%), and save history (6%). Clubs you already liked or joined are excluded.
3. **Platform Health & Improvement:** PostHog analytics and exception capture identify broken pages, error states, and high-interest search queries that yield no results.
4. **Safety & Policy Enforcement:** Preventing spam, preventing duplicate ratings, and enforcing Community Guidelines against harassment or doxxing.

## 8. Data Control, Rights & Deletion

- **Modifying / Deleting Reviews:** Users can edit or delete their own approved reviews directly from their profile. Editing moves the review back into `pending_reviews` under the same alias. Deleting permanently removes the review and triggers a recalculation of the club's ratings.
- **Updating Preferences:** Students can alter or wipe their majors, minors, current clubs, and interest tags at any time from `/profile`.
- **Toggling Likes & Saves:** Users can un-like or un-save clubs at any moment.
- **Account Deletion & Data Export:** To request a complete export of account data or permanent deletion of your profile and associated records, contact the team at `clubhouseucla@gmail.com`.
